How It Works

Simple steps to manage and access academic grades with ease.

1

Create Account and Login

Students use their student id to sign in using their email to access the grading dashboard.

2

Input & Review Grades

Teachers enter scores and assessments, while students can view and track their academic performance.

3

Generate Reports

Grades are automatically calculated and organized into downloadable summaries, ready for printing.
