Power Passed Down

Understanding how family dynasties shape Philippine politics.

What is a Political Dynasty?

A political dynasty occurs when multiple members of the same family hold public office, often across generations. In the Philippines, this practice has deep historical roots dating back to Spanish colonization, where elite families were granted power and land.

Impact on Governance

  • Concentration of Power: Dynasties dominate local and national politics, limiting opportunities for new leaders.
  • Corruption Risks: Studies show dynastic provinces often have higher poverty and weaker institutions.
  • Voter Manipulation: Name recall and patronage politics influence elections more than platforms.

Calls for Reform

Article II, Section 26 of the 1987 Constitution mandates the State to prohibit political dynasties, but no enabling law has been passed. Lawmakers and citizens continue to push for anti-dynasty legislation to ensure fair access to public service.
